Applications & Properties

Primary Applications
Protium (¹H) is the most abundant hydrogen isotope, comprising 99.98% of natural hydrogen. Used extensively in organic chemistry, petroleum refining, ammonia production for fertilizers, and as a clean fuel source in fuel cells.
